## 01 · Getting started and choosing the right workspace

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

Purpose: use one SlickHood account while keeping each business activity separate.

1. Open https://app.slickhood.com. Register if you are new, or sign in with your existing account. Follow an invitation when you are joining a particular home, tenancy, purchase or staff workspace.
2. Complete verification and the required onboarding steps shown on screen. Do not create another account simply because a role or property is missing.
3. Open Business Areas and choose the activity you intend to use. Check the active role displayed in the sidebar and profile header before creating or changing anything.
4. A Landlord works with rental inventory; Estate Management uses estate/service-charge inventory; Property Sale Management uses sale inventory. Selecting a different role is not permission to reclassify a property.
5. Select the appropriate workspace if you belong to several organisations. Staff access is limited by their assignment and resource scope.
6. Check the package for that business area. A package or add-on for one activity does not automatically enable another activity. Quotas, seats, features and current payment state can affect available actions.

Success looks like: the expected role, business area and workspace are visible and the sidebar contains the functions allowed for that context.

If a function is missing: confirm role and workspace, then package and staff assignment. Ask the owner or support to investigate with the page path and business area, not your password. Do not change account data to bypass a restriction.

## 02 · Registration, invitations and verification codes

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

Before starting: have access to your own email account and phone. Use your legal name for an individual; use the appropriate organisation option when registering a business.

1. If invited, open the most recent invitation from the intended sender. Review the property, unit or role presented. An invitation is tied to its intended email and purpose.
2. Enter your details and a strong, unique password; confirm it and read the applicable policies.
3. Submit once and follow the verification screen. Check the email spelling and mailbox, including spam or filtered folders. An email queued by the application is not proof it reached your inbox.
4. Enter the latest verification code in the verification screen only. Use the resend control after its waiting period if necessary. Older or expired codes may no longer work.
5. Continue through role selection, identity verification and any package step presented. An invitation may determine the role rather than offering public self-registration for it.
6. After registration, return to the same invitation if the linked home, tenancy or purchase is not yet shown.

If registration fails, do not repeatedly create accounts. Record the exact displayed message, approximate time and the page where it happened. Use Help to request human support. Staff should check the invitation status and notification delivery state rather than inventing a new identity.

Success looks like: the account advances to its next required step and, after onboarding, the correct dashboard or invited record becomes available. Registration alone does not prove KYC approval or an active paid package.

## 03 · Sign-in, Google, password reset and session recovery

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

1. Open Sign in and use the registered email and password. If Google sign-in is offered, select the intended Google account and complete Google's consent or challenge screens. Do not assume two different email addresses refer to the same SlickHood account.
2. Complete any additional verification requested. Allow the application to restore your current onboarding step before navigating away.
3. For a forgotten password, use Forgot password, enter the registered email and follow the reset instructions. Do not send a password to an administrator or ask them to read your current one.
4. After a reset, sign in again with the new password. Use one active session; signing in elsewhere may invalidate an older session.
5. For an expired-session message, save any non-sensitive notes you need, return to sign-in and reopen the intended page. Do not repeatedly retry a payment or document submission while authentication is failing.

If Google returns an error, record the visible error and which domain you used. Do not change API keys or browser security settings. Support must inspect the configured callback/domain and account state.

If the page remains on a welcome/loading message, use Help or report the page path and time. A browser error does not prove that your password caused the failure. Never share session cookies, authentication headers, OTPs or screenshots containing passwords.

## 04 · Uploading KYC documents and confirming OCR details

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

Prepare clear, complete originals belonging to the person or organisation registering. Test/sample documents must not be represented as genuine identity evidence.

1. Open the verification step and read each required document card. Requirements can combine across assigned roles and organisation type.
2. Choose the exact document type before uploading. National ID needs its front and back; a passport needs the passport document, not an ID back page. A KRA PIN certificate is required where shown, including tenant onboarding. Good Conduct is optional for base KYC, although a separate role/service may have additional requirements.
3. Upload a supported JPG, PNG or PDF within the limit displayed by the KYC form. The file must pass security scanning before acceptance into protected storage.
4. Inspect the key extracted fields, such as legal name, document number, birth date or tax PIN. Compare them against the original. Confirm correct information in the registrant confirmation fields; do not alter the source document to match an OCR error.
5. If OCR and your confirmed information differ, submit for authorised review. The original OCR reading and document remain evidence; a corrected value is not an automatic approval.
6. Complete all required cards and submit the case. Follow the resulting status and specific correction reasons.

Tenant-only cases may be automatically approved when their confirmed data matches OCR and all upload/verification checks pass. Accounts with other roles still require admin approval. A tenant role on a multi-role account does not automatically qualify.

Upload success, OCR complete and KYC approved are different states. Confirm the final case status before assuming activation.

## 05 · Recovering from document rejection or upload failure

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

Read the exact message before replacing anything.

• Security scanning temporarily unavailable: the application could not safely complete its scanner check. Do not disable antivirus or keep uploading copies. Retry later and report the time/page to support.
• Unsupported media type or size: use a genuine supported format and the displayed size limit. Renaming a file extension does not convert the document.
• Replace or upload a professional/business certificate or appointment letter: this refers to a separate required card, not necessarily a failure of the ID already saved. Read the requirements for your assigned role and organisation type.
• Saved — reviewer confirmation needed: the upload exists but a field mismatch or document check needs review. Confirm the accurate key fields and explain the discrepancy through the designated verification workflow.
• Name mismatch: compare the legal account name with the original. Trading names and personal names are not interchangeable identity evidence.
• ID front/back mismatch: ensure both sides belong to the same document and person. Do not upload a different person's back page to complete the card.

Use View protected original to confirm which version is stored. Use Replace this document for a genuine replacement; do not create another account. Retain the displayed rejection reason and resubmit only the requested correction.

For a persistent problem, provide support with document type, approximate upload time and the visible error, not your full identity number or an ID attachment in chat. Support should inspect the protected KYC workflow.

## 06 · Packages, add-ons, renewals and access

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

1. Choose the intended business area before opening Upgrade Plan or the package selection step.
2. Compare the displayed price, currency, billing period, quotas, staff seats and included functions. Similar Bronze, Silver or Gold names in different business areas are not the same product.
3. Select only the package for the activity you intend to operate. Separately purchased add-ons and the primary package contribute to effective access; the backend remains authoritative.
4. Review the invoice and use the payment route offered for that invoice. A free plan and a sales-managed/custom quote are different: custom pricing must not be treated as zero-cost activation.
5. After payment initiation, check the invoice and subscription state. A browser return or payment prompt alone is not payment confirmation.
6. For renewal, use the current renewal or upgrade journey and check the resulting term and invoice. Do not pay repeatedly because a button still appears.

Retiring a plan from new sales should not immediately remove a customer's already-paid, unexpired term. Expiry, cancellation or a different business area can still affect access.

Important limitation: scheduled subscription processing does not itself prove that a payment provider will charge you automatically. Do not rely on auto-debit unless an actual provider mandate and charge arrangement have been confirmed. For changes, custom quotes or payment exceptions not available in the UI, ask support.

Success looks like: the intended product and term are active, with the expected allowed functions and quotas, and the invoice has a confirmed payment state.

## 07 · Landlord: create a rental property and similar units

Audience: Landlord,WorkspaceAdmin,PropertyManager,LeasingOfficer,Superadmin,Support.

Before setup: choose the Landlord rental workspace, complete verification, and have an eligible package and unit allowance.

1. Open Properties → All Properties and create the property. Enter the required description, location, currency and property type. Rental inventory must use the rental workflow, not estate service charges or sale.
2. Save once. Open the saved property before creating it again if navigation fails. A page-load error does not prove that the save failed.
3. Create a unit under that property. Choose an allowed unit type, a unique unit reference, size/unit of measurement, image where required, rent and applicable charges.
4. Save and confirm the new unit appears under the correct property.
5. To generate similar units, start from the existing unit and use the similar-unit action available to your role. Supply the requested quantity and naming/reference pattern, and inspect the generated references before confirming.
6. Confirm the resulting unit count, references and shared characteristics. Similar-unit generation is for separate inventory records; it must not copy a tenant's identity or a signed lease into new units.
7. Configure the landlord's own verified receiving account before invoicing or inviting a tenant into a payment-dependent process.

If unit types or properties are empty, check property classification, workspace assignment, property-type catalogue mappings and package access. Do not substitute a sale property to bypass an empty rental dropdown. Report the property reference and error to support.

Success looks like: each intended unit is listed once under the rental property with its own reference and correct pricing.

## 08 · Landlord and tenant: invitation to draft lease

Audience: Landlord,Tenant,PropertyManager,LeasingOfficer,WorkspaceAdmin,Superadmin,Support.

Landlord steps:
1. Open the intended vacant rental unit. Enter the prospective tenant's correct email and use the invitation action.
2. Check the invitation record. Queued means the system accepted the email job, not that the tenant has opened or received it.
3. If necessary, check spelling and use the supported resend flow. Do not forward a different tenant's email-bound invitation.
4. Prepare the required rental terms and approved lease template. Ensure the correct receiving account and charges are configured.

Tenant steps:
1. Open the emailed link and review the property and unit. Do not continue if it is not the home you expected.
2. Use the initialize/continue lease action. If not registered, follow registration with the invited email, complete verification and return to the invitation.
3. Complete required tenant KYC, including the KRA PIN certificate and confirmation of extracted details. Autoapproval is conditional, not a bypass for mismatches or unsafe files.
4. Access the tenant dashboard and inspect the draft lease and its approval/signature status.
5. Check the parties, unit, rent, deposit, other charges, dates and obligations before signing. Ask for correction of inaccurate terms before executing the document.

Tenants view their assigned tenancy and documents. They should not manage the landlord's lease templates or browse the landlord's All Properties inventory.

Success looks like: the invitation resolves to the intended unit and tenant, and a readable draft is available with an accurate status. Initialization alone does not mean the tenancy is active.

## 09 · Lease review, signing, occupancy and notices

Audience: Landlord,Tenant,PropertyManager,LeasingOfficer,WorkspaceAdmin,Superadmin,Support.

1. Open Leases → Lease operations or the tenancy's document link. Landlords may maintain approved templates through Lease templates; tenants use the draft/document view.
2. Open the protected PDF. Check the exact property and unit, parties, amounts, currency, dates, obligations and document version.
3. If the viewer cannot render, use its download fallback. A signed-in authenticated download is expected; do not put an access token in a copied URL.
4. The tenant reviews and signs the issued lease when satisfied. The landlord reviews and countersigns.
5. Only after the required signatures and activation checks succeed should the lease become active and the unit become occupied. A draft, one signature or a failed signing request is not enough.
6. Reopen the executed document to verify both signature timestamps and the final terms. Keep the document history; do not overwrite a signed snapshot when terms need changing.
7. Use Invoices and Payments to track the agreed charges and confirmed receipts.

For termination, renewal or a disputed charge, follow the governed lease/document actions and ask an authorised person to review the applicable terms. A reminder is not automatically a legally effective termination notice. Do not delete a lease or tenant record to clear an unpaid balance.

If status appears inconsistent, refresh and report the lease reference and the last action. Support should check the stored document and tenancy state without manually pretending that a signature occurred.

## 10 · Estate manager: estate setup and homeowner onboarding

Audience: EstateManager,EstateOperationsManager,WorkspaceAdmin,PropertyManager,Superadmin,Support.

Before setup: activate the Estate Management business area and package, not the rental or sale package.

1. Create or choose the estate property using SERVICE_CHARGE inventory. Set up its homes/units in the correct estate workspace.
2. Open Estate Payment Setup and attach the estate's own verified operating/receiving account.
3. Review estate setup readiness: homes, payment account, homeowners and an approved current-year budget where required.
4. From the intended home, enter the homeowner's email and send an invitation. A homeowner invitation establishes ownership participation; it is not a staff invitation.
5. The invited person completes registration and KYC using the intended email, then accesses My Home.
6. Check the exact ownership record and start date. For someone who owns several homes, match the ownership record and unit, not just the person's name.
7. Prepare the residential agreement from that ownership context. Do not use a rental lease to stand in for an estate agreement.
8. Maintain ownership history when a home changes hands. Record the end/transfer reason and retain prior evidence; do not erase the old owner to make a new one appear.

If the property dropdown is empty, verify SERVICE_CHARGE classification and workspace scope. Older rental-default records need a deliberate classification review, not arbitrary bulk relabelling.

Success looks like: each home has the correct current ownership, the homeowner can see only authorised homes, and estate agreements and charges refer to the right ownership/unit.

## 11 · Homeowner: agreement, charges and estate participation

Audience: Homeowner,EstateManager,EstateOperationsManager,WorkspaceAdmin,Superadmin,Support.

1. Open the homeowner invitation and confirm the estate and home. Register or sign in with the invited email and finish verification.
2. Open My Home and select the correct home if you own more than one.
3. Use View agreements. The list should be filtered to the selected property/unit while retaining your authorised document history.
4. Review the Estate Residential Agreement PDF: parties, home, ownership context, effective date and recorded charge/schedule.
5. Sign only after the manager has issued the approved document and you understand the terms. Either original party may sign first for an estate agreement.
6. When both signatures are recorded, the agreement is completed. This does not create a rental tenancy or a property-sales reservation.
7. Open the linked invoices to review service charges, currency, due dates and confirmed payments. Use the estate's receiving account shown by checkout.
8. Review approved budgets, meetings and estate communications available to you. Report maintenance through the appropriate estate workflow.

Manager operations include budget approval/closure, meeting quorum and minutes, and evidence-backed work-order completion. Entering a budget actual is not the same as reconciling an expense ledger.

If ownership ends, historical signed evidence and obligations must not disappear. You may retain original-party access to historical documents without permission to create new agreements for that home. For a disputed charge or ownership change, seek human review; the chatbot cannot decide it.

## 12 · Sales manager: inventory, listings and buyer invitation

Audience: SalesAgent,SalesCoordinator,ListingAgent,WorkspaceAdmin,Superadmin,Support.

1. Select Property Sale Management and its eligible package. Set up SALE properties and units in the authorised workspace.
2. Use Sales Payment Setup for the sales recipient's verified account. SlickHood's subscription account is not automatically the seller's receiving account.
3. Prepare the listing with accurate property details, currency, price, images and location. Follow the listing moderation process where required.
4. Inspect the saved listing and availability. A reserved/completed unit should not still be presented as freely available inventory.
5. Open Property Sale Management, record the prospective sale and enter the buyer's email. Send the buyer invitation.
6. Ask the buyer to open the latest link and complete registration/KYC with that email. A buyer-addressed letter cannot be generated against an unfinished or unrelated invitation.
7. Progress the permitted lead/viewing/offer steps and record a positive offer amount before preparing the letter of offer.

If properties or units are missing, confirm SALE classification, package and workspace scope. Do not create rental inventory just to populate a dropdown. If a save succeeds but the detail page fails, refresh or return to the list and inspect the saved record before trying another create.

Success looks like: one correctly classified available unit, one appropriate active sale, the intended buyer linked through the invitation, and an accurate offer amount/currency. Listing publication is not a legal guarantee of ownership or a completed sale.

## 13 · Buyer and seller: letter of offer and document viewing

Audience: Buyer,SalesAgent,SalesCoordinator,WorkspaceAdmin,Superadmin,Support.

1. The manager selects the sale, exact offer amount/currency and a future response deadline, then generates a versioned draft using an approved template.
2. Review the frozen draft. Creating or viewing it does not reserve the property.
3. The authorised issuer issues the letter. An unreviewed legal template must not be forced through approval.
4. The buyer opens My Property Purchase and the sale's letters/agreement link. The manager can use the same sale-scoped document route.
5. Open the protected PDF or download fallback. Check the unit, parties, price, currency, response deadline and conditions.
6. Sign only when the issued version is correct. Either original party may sign first for the offer process.
7. When both required signatures are recorded, the normal flow reserves the sale. A separate acceptance click is not required by that flow.
8. Reopen the letter after reservation to check both signature times and retain the evidence.

Expired or cancelled letters remain historical evidence but cannot be signed to advance the sale. If wording or price needs changing, use a controlled new version; do not edit the signed snapshot.

A signed letter of offer is not a signed sale agreement, legal title transfer, proof of funding or handover. Continue through the separate due-diligence, agreement, invoice and transfer milestones. Ask an authorised legal or sales representative about the meaning of contractual terms.

## 14 · Sale agreement, buyer billing and completion

Audience: Buyer,SalesAgent,SalesCoordinator,PropertyAccountant,WorkspaceAdmin,Superadmin,Support.

1. After reservation, complete the required due-diligence steps and supporting evidence. Use the separate sale agreement workflow; the offer letter cannot satisfy the sale-agreement milestone.
2. Review, issue and sign the approved agreement through its governed document process. Preserve versions and original-party access.
3. The authorised operator creates the linked buyer invoice using the correct sale, buyer, amount, currency and verified payee account.
4. The buyer follows the Billing shortcut for that invoice, checks its reference and recipient, then pays using the method actually offered.
5. Wait for confirmed/reconciled payment. A typed payment reference, proof upload or browser redirect is not enough to mark funding complete.
6. Record the required transfer-registration and handover evidence. Complete only when the agreement, funding and remaining milestones satisfy the backend checks.
7. Review the final sale and ownership state. Previous ownership history must remain available rather than being deleted.

An invoice labelled escrow does not prove a regulated escrow arrangement or external registry verification. Funding can be rechecked at completion; a refunded or unpaid invoice must not rely on an old funded marker.

If a payment or completion step fails, keep the existing sale/invoice and ask support to inspect the reference. Do not create a second sale, alter a buyer identity or manually set a paid state to force completion.

## 15 · Receiving payments: configure your own account

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

The cardinal rule is that each recipient configures their own receiving account for their activity. SlickHood receives platform subscription payments; a landlord, estate, seller or merchant receives its own business payments.

1. Open the relevant workspace and payment setup page: Payment Setup for rentals, Estate Payment Setup, Sales Payment Setup, or Merchant Accounts for Services/Soko. Platform administrators use SlickHood Accounts for platform receipts.
2. Create or maintain the account using the correct owner, purpose, currency and provider/channel details requested.
3. Enter provider credentials only in the protected configuration form. Do not paste them into Help Desk, ordinary notes or screenshots.
4. Complete the required verification. An account's existence is not evidence it is active, correctly owned or ready to receive payments.
5. Attach the verified account to the relevant property, estate, shop or service workflow.
6. Use a designated test invoice to verify the full provider journey, callback and resulting recipient/invoice state before advertising a route as ready.

Direct M-Pesa, bank-paybill/indirect M-Pesa, PesaLink and Paystack depend on actual provider configuration and availability. Do not assume every method appears for every invoice.
For a bank paybill, use the bank's specified paybill and account/invoice reference format. A message to the customer's phone alone does not tell SlickHood which invoice to reconcile; the configured confirmation route must supply enough matching information.

Only authorised maintainers should replace credentials or change a destination. After a change, recheck verification and attachment. Never switch another owner's destination or share one merchant's secret across unrelated users.

## 16 · Pay an invoice and check the receipt

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

1. Open Invoices or the contextual billing link from your lease, purchase, estate, order or booking.
2. Open the invoice and verify its owner/recipient, purpose, amount, currency, due date and outstanding balance. Do not pay an unfamiliar recipient.
3. Choose Pay now and select a method enabled for that invoice. Follow the provider's prompt or redirect using the reference shown.
4. Enter a payment authorisation code only into the provider's official interface. Never provide it to SlickHood Help or a merchant in chat.
5. Return to the existing invoice and refresh. Payment initiation, awaiting confirmation and paid are different states.
6. Open Payments and the invoice/receipt details to confirm the successful amount and reference. Keep your provider receipt for reconciliation.
7. If payment failed before completion, retry from the existing invoice. If money was deducted but the invoice remains pending, contact support before paying again.

For bank-paybill payments, include the exact account/invoice reference required by the configured bank route. For manually submitted evidence, wait for verification; proof uploaded is not paid.

Refund requested, refund approved, settlement recorded and money returned are also different. An internal status change does not prove that a provider transferred money. Report a dispute with the invoice/order reference and non-sensitive timing information. The chatbot cannot approve refunds, change receiving accounts or decide whether a disputed payment is valid.

Success looks like: the correct invoice is reconciled to the correct payment, amount, currency and intended recipient without duplicate payment.

## 17 · Soko merchant: shop, products and fulfilment

Audience: ServiceProvider,Superadmin,Support.

1. Open Merchant Accounts and configure your own verified receiving account.
2. Open Services & Shopping → Marketplace → Soko. Create your shop with contact information, location, pickup/delivery options and delivery fee.
3. Attach the correct account. If the shop already exists, edit it rather than creating a duplicate.
4. Submit shop for review. This is a review request, not immediate publication. Follow any rejection reason.
5. Add products, prices, stock and supported scanned images. Confirm the saved draft if an image upload fails; repair the draft instead of duplicating the product.
6. Publish eligible products under the approved shop and maintain prices/stock through editing.
7. Review orders after confirmed payment, then confirm and pack them.
8. For pickup, use the ready-for-pickup and pickup-completion flow. For delivery, assign the rider/courier, record the Nairobi ETA, dispatch, upload private delivery proof, then enter the customer's completion code.
9. If the code is wrong, keep the successful proof upload and retry the code appropriately. Repeated wrong attempts can block completion; never bypass the customer's confirmation.
10. Handle eligible cancellations and finance requests through the authorised process.

Soko supports merchant-arranged riders/couriers; do not promise an independent SlickHood live-GPS logistics network. Refund and settlement records are not automatic external transfers.

Success looks like: the approved shop shows accurate inventory, orders use your attached payee account, and completion follows the correct pickup or delivery safeguards.

## 18 · Soko customer: checkout, delivery and problems

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

1. Open Marketplace → Soko and find the intended shop and product.
2. Review product details, price, currency, stock and fulfilment options. Build a cart for the intended shop.
3. Choose pickup or delivery, check the delivery fee and total, and confirm the destination where required.
4. Create the order once. Pay its existing invoice using the merchant's enabled receiving route.
5. If payment initiation fails, use Pay now on that order/invoice. Refresh orders to inspect the current state instead of creating another order.
6. Track confirmation, packing and either pickup readiness or dispatch.
7. For delivery, receive and inspect the goods before giving the courier the six-digit delivery confirmation code. This code is for the delivery workflow; never share a payment PIN or bank OTP.
8. If a code attempt fails, the merchant should retry safely without repeatedly uploading the same proof.
9. Use the supported cancellation action for an eligible state and give a clear reason. A paid cancellation may create a refund request; wait for verified refund evidence.

A reservation can expire while an order is unpaid. If you paid near expiry, contact support with the order and invoice references before trying again.

For missing, unsafe or incorrect goods, report the issue promptly. Do not mark receipt merely because a courier requests a code. The Help Desk can route the case but cannot declare that a refund has been transferred.

## 19 · Services: provider onboarding and customer booking

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

Provider:
1. Open My Services and complete the profile, service/category, required verification documents and referees where requested.
2. Use clear genuine documents and resolve review feedback. Upload success does not make a service approved or listed.
3. Attach your own verified merchant receiving account and submit for review.
4. Accept an appropriate booking and create its invoice through the workflow.
5. Start work only after the required payment confirmation. Record progress and completion evidence; do not claim completion merely to unlock settlement.

Customer:
1. Open Marketplace → Services, choose an available provider and review the service details.
2. Select a future appointment in Nairobi time and enter accurate job/location details.
3. Confirm the booking once and follow acceptance and invoice status.
4. Use Pay now on the booking invoice, then check confirmed payment. A provider buying another provider's service should still see their customer-side actions for that booking.
5. Track start and completion, inspect the result, and rate completed work where available.
6. Cancel only through an eligible action and provide a reason. Report fulfilment, safety, refund or quality disputes through the appropriate complaint/support channel.

Listing, payment, completion and settlement are separate decisions. A provider's active status and document review matter throughout the journey. Do not send private access credentials in Help Desk messages or assume every payment automatically executes a refund/payout.

## 20 · My Wealth: assets, valuations, liabilities and goals

Audience: AssetPortfolioManager,Superadmin,Support.

1. Open My Wealth in the authorised wealth role and select the display currency.
2. Add an asset using an available catalogue category. Enter a clear name, ownership details, currency, acquisition information and valuation date/value.
3. Choose manual valuation by default. Use market pricing only for an eligible instrument/category and supported symbol. Eligibility is not a guarantee the market-data provider covers that instrument.
4. If linking an existing property, select only a property you are authorised to link; a link is not permission to inspect another owner's asset.
5. Record income and expenses with accurate date, currency and amount. Review their effect on cash flow without confusing them with current asset value.
6. Add liabilities and keep their outstanding balances current. Net worth depends on both asset values and debts.
7. Set goals and review projections as estimates, not guaranteed returns. Review dates and exchange-rate availability when comparing currencies.
8. Use the protected vault for supporting documents within the displayed limit. Files must pass scanning; share neither vault links nor identity documents in chat.
9. Refresh quotes where available and inspect valuation history. Stale data or failed conversion should be shown as unavailable, not a fabricated zero portfolio.
10. Maintain records through edits/history. If a save fails, keep your draft and retry after checking the error.

The dashboard is a recordkeeping and planning aid, not a valuation certificate or investment recommendation. Ask a qualified person about tax, legal ownership or investment suitability.

## 21 · Insurance: quote to policy, renewal and claim

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

1. Open Insurance Hub and submit a request for the relevant cover using accurate risk details and the required protected documents.
2. Track the request while authorised insurance staff review it. A request is not cover and an indicative amount is not a binding policy.
3. Review the offered quotes and insurer, cover scope, exclusions, excess, premium and dates. Ask an authorised adviser if anything is unclear.
4. Select the intended quote through the application. Use the premium payment instructions/account linked to that transaction.
5. Upload premium evidence only where the protected workflow requests it. Wait for payment verification; proof submitted is not proof of an issued policy.
6. Open the issued policy and confirm policyholder, insurer, insured risk, cover period and documents.
7. For renewal, review the expiry notice and current renewal terms, then follow Pay Now where offered. A reminder or payment initiation alone does not extend cover.
8. For a claim, use the claim workflow and provide accurate incident details and requested evidence. Track review status and requests for further information.

Silverwood's authorised insurance operations team handles review and policy decisions. Insurer brands identify insurers; SlickHood's platform role is not the same as underwriting insurance.

For urgent claims, disputed premiums, coverage interpretation or a policy not received after payment, request human support with the case/reference. Do not send identity numbers, payment secrets or medical/private documents in ordinary chat. The chatbot cannot bind cover, accept a claim or give a definitive coverage decision.

## 22 · Affiliate referrals, commissions and payout tracking

Audience: Affiliate,Superadmin,Support.

1. Open Affiliate and use your assigned referral link or code.
2. Share it accurately so registration and qualifying activity can be attributed. Do not create artificial registrations or promise commissions outside the current programme terms.
3. Review the referrals and their qualifying-payment status. A click or registration is not automatically a payable commission.
4. Check the commission's amount, eligibility and holding/maturity state. Use the current terms shown by the platform; do not infer a lifetime percentage from an old message.
5. After eligible funds mature and the payout threshold is met, request a payout using your authorised receiving details.
6. Track the administrator's decision and the recorded payout evidence. Requested, approved and externally paid are distinct states.
7. If attribution or payment appears missing, report the referral/payout reference and approximate date. Do not share a customer's private identity information or provider credentials.

Administrators review qualifying payments, maturity and payout conditions before deciding a request. Reversals or disqualified underlying transactions can affect eligibility; a dashboard estimate is not a guarantee of disbursement.

Success looks like: an attributable eligible referral progresses through the applicable payment/holding conditions to a reviewed payout, with a verifiable record of the actual transfer. Ask human support to investigate disputes rather than asking the AI to change balances or approve a payout.

## 23 · Community Funds: contributions and expenditure

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

Manager:
1. Create the fund in the authorised context with its purpose, currency and correctly owned verified receiving account.
2. Open/enrol the fund according to the available workflow and review participating members.
3. Review contribution invoices and confirmed receipts separately from pledges.
4. Record expenditure requests with purpose, amount and supporting reference/evidence. Follow the required approval decision before recording any disbursement.
5. When an external payment has actually been made, record its evidence accurately. Record external payment does not instruct a bank or wallet to transfer funds automatically.

Member:
1. Open Community Funds and choose the fund you are allowed to access.
2. Review purpose, currency, membership and contribution details.
3. Make the pledge/contribution and pay the generated invoice through the fund's verified account.
4. Refresh the fund and invoice to confirm receipt. A pledge, pending invoice or partial/mismatched receipt must not be mistaken for a fully paid contribution.
5. Review the transaction information allowed to your role. Other members' private receipt references should not be exposed.

Keep currencies separate when comparing balances. If expenditure or contribution submission fails, retain the draft and check the current record before resubmitting. For a disputed allocation, refund, membership or disbursement, request authorised human review. Do not edit balances or mark an external payment solely to make a dashboard reconcile.

## 24 · Tax Assist: estimates, history and limits

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

Tax Assist helps organise calculation inputs and results. It is not proof of a submitted return, paid tax, accepted KRA filing or professional tax advice.

1. Open Tax Assist and choose the supported calculation relevant to your circumstances.
2. Enter the requested period, amounts and facts accurately. Do not mix currencies or omit material facts simply to obtain a result.
3. Review the calculation, warnings and rule/version information. A rule may depend on effective date and eligibility.
4. For residential rental calculations, inspect assessed tax and any eligible credit rather than treating a credit as a refund automatically.
5. For property gains, do not infer a deadline solely from the calculation date. The application may lack full payment, registration or lodgement facts needed to determine timing safely.
6. Save or review calculation history. Historical results should show their original rule snapshot rather than silently changing when an administrator edits current rules.
7. Obtain qualified advice and verify requirements through the relevant official tax channels before acting on filing, deadlines, exemptions or payment.

A connection request or a saved PIN reference is not proof of a live KRA integration. Do not provide a tax portal password or OTP to the Help Desk.

If the service is paused, unavailable or a rule looks wrong, keep the inputs and calculation reference and request human review. The chatbot should explain navigation and limitations, not make a final tax determination.

## 25 · Visitors and Smart Gates

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

Host/homeowner/tenant:
1. Open Visitors in the correct authorised property or home.
2. Register the visit with accurate visitor details, arrival/validity window and vehicle information where needed.
3. Review pending requests and approve or deny only visits you are entitled to manage.
4. Share the permitted invitation/access credential only with the intended visitor and cancel it if the visit no longer applies.
5. Expired, cancelled or inactive credentials should not be reused.

Guard/security staff:
1. Select the assigned property and inspect the expected or walk-in visit.
2. Follow host approval and valid check-in/check-out rules. Do not override an expired request merely because a visitor presents an old message.
3. Record actual movements accurately; check vehicle and direction where required.
4. Escalate disputed or unsafe access to the responsible human security team.

Smart Gate administrators:
1. Register and maintain only controllers for the authorised property.
2. Enter the controller's public key in the controller form; keep the private signing key on the device.
3. Test signed decisions, expiry, revocation and replay handling with the installer before relying on a physical barrier.
4. Review access logs and disable compromised devices promptly through the authorised workflow.

An application approval does not certify the physical gate, network or emergency egress arrangements. Follow the site's independent emergency procedures; do not rely on the chatbot during a safety emergency.

## 26 · Documents, notifications, reports and dashboard checks

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

Documents:
1. Open Documents & Notices or the record's contextual document link.
2. Check the property/home/sale and document status before opening the protected original or PDF.
3. Use the authenticated viewer/download fallback. Do not share temporary links or screenshots containing private documents.
4. Preserve executed versions. A replacement, draft or uploaded proof does not itself change an agreement's legal/approval status.

Notifications:
1. Open Notifications and review the related record, not just the subject line.
2. Distinguish queued, sent and delivered states where shown. Check your email address/phone and spam filtering if expected messages are absent.
3. Verify late-payment and termination-related notices against the lease and authorised process. A reminder is not automatically a legally effective termination.
4. WhatsApp availability must not be assumed; the integration remains subject to secure configuration and rollout.

Reports and dashboards:
1. Check active role/workspace, then choose the relevant report and its date mode: historical activity, a snapshot, or forward-looking expiry.
2. Generate deliberately and review refresh time, warnings and partial failures. Missing data is not a confirmed zero balance.
3. Screen and export limits can truncate results; read the warning before relying on totals or an export.
4. Keep different currencies separate and protect downloaded CSVs.
5. Use the underlying invoices, documents and records to investigate discrepancies.

Report a problem with its page, record reference, selected dates and time. Never include passwords, keys or full identity/payment data.

## 27 · Help Desk: ask a question and reach a person

Audience: All users, including registration guests.

1. Open the floating Help button on registration or other supported screens. Signed-in users can also open Help Desk → My help.
2. Describe one issue clearly: what you were trying to do, the page, the visible error and a non-sensitive record reference. Never send a password, verification code, payment PIN, API key, full card number or identity document number.
3. Read the answer and its article references. The assistant provides guidance from published, role-visible knowledge; it cannot inspect or change your private business records, sign agreements, approve KYC or confirm a payment on your behalf.
4. Choose Talk to a person/Human support if the issue is unresolved or concerns a dispute, account access, KYC/legal decision or safety.
5. Once handed off, follow the ticket status. You may add safe details; the conversation stays in the human-support process rather than repeatedly restarting the bot.
6. On the full Help Desk page, use Refresh Help Desk to see updates. An agent reply can also trigger an email for a registered user; actual delivery depends on notification infrastructure.
7. When resolved, use Reopen case in My help if the same issue persists. Keep the ticket number.
8. Use Knowledge and its search field for approved guidance. Article numbers in answers identify supporting articles; drafts are not customer guidance.

Guest help is tied to a short-lived browser session/token. Register/sign in in the same browser session to link it to your account. If it expires, start a new guest case and quote the old ticket number without sharing the guest token.

## 28 · Support staff: triage, claim, reply and resolve

Audience: Support,Superadmin.

1. Sign in with an authorised Support or Superadmin role and open Help Desk → Support queue.
2. Inspect waiting, unassigned, breached-response-target and waiting-for-customer counts. Use Previous cases/Next cases and Refresh Help Desk to navigate rather than assuming the first page contains every ticket.
3. Open the case, review the conversation and context, then Claim case. Do not take over another agent's case unless your role explicitly permits it.
4. Separate customer communication from staff-only work. Tick Internal note for a private support note; untick it before sending a customer reply.
5. Keep credentials and identity/payment secrets out of both replies and internal notes. Use authorised protected record screens for investigation.
6. Send a clear reply with the observed problem, safe next step and any information the customer must provide. A reply moves the case to waiting for the customer; do not promise email delivery solely because the reply saved.
7. When the customer responds, the ticket returns to waiting for support. The chatbot must not resume independent answers after human handoff.
8. Resolve only after the issue is adequately addressed. A customer can reopen a resolved case.
9. Escalate payment disputes, legal/KYC decisions, security incidents and emergencies to the responsible authorised team. Claiming a case does not confer authority to change balances or approve identities.

Response targets are configured operational timers, not a guarantee of instant staffing. Repeat escalation should not push an existing deadline later or downgrade priority. SLA alerts require working notifications and monitored recipients.

## 29 · Administrator: daily controls and exception handling

Audience: Superadmin.

1. Open the admin dashboard and use the function directory to reach Users & Staff, KYC Reviews, catalogue maintenance, recipient accounts, operational queues, reports and configuration.
2. Review the active role and permissions. A menu link is not a substitute for backend authorisation.
3. In KYC Reviews, compare the protected original, OCR extraction and registrant-confirmed fields. Resolve discrepancies with documented reasons. Tenant-only autoapproval is conditional; other roles require admin review.
4. In the subscription catalogue, search the correct business area and billing period. Preserve historical plans/invoices; retire obsolete offers rather than deleting payment history. Custom/sales-managed is not free. The catalogue is not a complete subscriber exception-management workbench.
5. In recipient-account maintenance, verify owner, purpose and provider attachment. Do not use the platform's subscription account as every landlord/estate/merchant's destination.
6. Review Services/Soko moderation, insurance operations and affiliate decisions using their own authorised workspaces. A moderation or finance decision is not an external payment.
7. Review notification failures, Help Desk queue/SLA state and operational reports.
8. Use Global Config only with the necessary permission. Sensitive values are replacement-only; do not expose existing secrets or store them in browser notes.
9. Check audit evidence and escalate inconsistent records rather than manually fabricating successful actions.

Before releasing new functionality, verify the actual provider, document and role-isolation journeys on designated test records. A green dashboard or passing mocked test does not certify live settlement, message delivery, legal templates or physical gate safety.

## 30 · Super Admin: publish and maintain the manual knowledge

Audience: Superadmin.

1. Open Help Desk → Knowledge using an account with article-management permission.
2. Select Import user manual drafts. This adds only missing packaged chapters. It does not overwrite existing articles, delete history or publish content automatically.
3. Read each draft against the current deployed screens and enabled integrations. The packaged manual describes the audited release candidate; publish a chapter only when its covered functionality is available.
4. Check title, category, searchable keywords and audience. A blank audience makes the article available to all audiences, including registration guests.
5. For restricted material, use exact role names supported by the backend, such as Landlord, Tenant, EstateManager, Homeowner, SalesAgent, Buyer, ServiceProvider, AssetPortfolioManager, Support or Superadmin. Do not use friendly sidebar labels as invented role codes.
6. Keep administration and internal-support instructions restricted to the appropriate staff. Never put customer records or secrets in any article.
7. Tick Publish for customers and AI grounding, then Save article. Test retrieval from an ordinary permitted role and from a role that must not see it.
8. Search a representative customer question and verify the answer follows the published chapter and correctly hands off sensitive decisions.
9. When a process changes, edit/review the article deliberately or unpublish it. Importing the same manual again intentionally retains existing content; it is not an overwrite/update tool.
10. Keep a release/reviewer record outside the article body where your organisation records approvals.

The AI consumes only published active articles visible to its conversation role. Knowledge content is reference data, never authority to bypass security, perform payments or approve accounts.

## Glossary and escalation checklist

- **Workspace:** the organisation/resource scope in which you act.
- **Business area:** rental, estate, sales or another separate activity.
- **Payee:** the intended invoice-payment recipient.
- **OCR:** machine-extracted text, not identity approval.
- **Issued:** a controlled document available to its parties, not necessarily signed.
- **Reconciled:** verified payment state, not a prompt or proof upload.
- **Draft knowledge:** excluded from customer and AI guidance until published.

Report the page path, business area, non-sensitive record reference, approximate time, steps and visible error. Redact screenshots. Never send passwords, OTPs, PINs, keys, guest tokens, identity numbers or full card data. Use local emergency procedures for immediate danger. Financial, legal, identity and security decisions require authorised human review.
